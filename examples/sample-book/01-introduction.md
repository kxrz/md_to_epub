This chapter has no `#` heading: its title comes from the front matter,
so it still shows up as a proper chapter in the table of contents.

Paragraphs are typeset like a printed book, with justified text, hyphenation
and indented first lines. The language is detected automatically, which gives
your e-reader the right hyphenation rules.

## What you can write

Everything Markdown offers works: *italics*, **bold**, ==highlights==,
`inline code`, footnotes[^1], links to <https://pandoc.org> and more.

[^1]: Footnotes become pop-up notes on most e-readers.
